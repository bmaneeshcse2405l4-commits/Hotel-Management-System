## 📌 About the Project

The **Hotel Management System** is a software application developed to simplify and automate the daily operations of a hotel.

It helps manage:

- 👤 Customer details
- 🏨 Room information
- 📅 Room bookings
- 🛎️ Check-in and check-out
- 💳 Billing and payments
- 📊 Reports and records

The main goal of this project is to **reduce manual work, minimize errors, save time, and improve hotel management efficiency**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To automate hotel management activities.
- To manage customer information efficiently.
- To maintain room availability records.
- To simplify room booking and cancellation.
- To generate accurate bills.
- To reduce paperwork and human errors.
- To provide an easy-to-use management system.

## 📂 Main Modules

### 👤 Customer Management
- Add customer details
- Update customer information
- Search customer records
- Delete customer records

### 🏨 Room Management
- Add rooms
- Update room details
- Check room availability
- Manage room status

### 📅 Booking Management
- Book rooms
- Cancel bookings
- View booking details
- Check booking history

### 🛎️ Check-in & Check-out
- Record customer check-in
- Record customer check-out
- Update room availability

### 💳 Billing & Payment
- Calculate room charges
- Generate customer bills
- Record payment information

### 📊 Reports
- Customer reports
- Booking reports
- Room availability reports
- Payment reports
